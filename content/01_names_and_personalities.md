# Names & Personalities

Two separate pools that the game combines: a **name** (with a nickname) and a **personality**.
Any personality can land on any race — the race pools below are only for *names*, and the
personality list at the bottom is shared. That way one authored personality gets reused across
dozens of characters instead of being locked to one.

**Personalities are always a trade.** If you write one that is purely good, it is a stat bonus
wearing a costume. Every entry below costs something.

## Personalities (14)

Each has: a **battle effect** or an **out-of-battle effect**, and a **cost**. The `key` is what
the game uses.

| key | Name | What it does | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stubborn` | Stubborn | +8 morale recovered every quiet turn | — (weak effect, no cost) |
| `thrice` | Widowed thrice | +17% damage vs anything under half hitpoints | — |
| `counter` | Counts aloud | +8 to hit on any turn they did not move | Only pays if you stand still |
| `windtouched` | Wind-touched | Spells cost 5 fewer Winds | −2 MOR |
| `twitchy` | Twitchy | +2 AGI | −3 MOR |
| `vegetarian` | Insists he is a vegetarian | +6 MOR | −10% damage dealt |
| `greedy` | Light-fingered | +15% crowns from every event reward | −4 MOR (nobody trusts them) |
| `kind` | Soft-hearted | +6 MOR to everyone within 2 hexes at battle start | −1 STR |
| `grim` | Grim | Immune to the morale hit from an ally dying | −6 starting morale |
| `braggart` | Braggart | +10 morale on their own kill (on top of the usual) | −8 morale when they miss twice running |
| `cautious` | Cautious | +6 dodge while below half hitpoints | −5 to hit while above it |
| `veteran` | Old hand | +4 to hit, +4 dodge | −1 AGI (bad knees) |
| `feral` | Feral | +12% damage while their own morale is Wavering or worse | −4 RESOLVE equivalent (breaks sooner) |
| `dutiful` | Dutiful | +8 dodge while standing next to the Captain | −4 dodge when not |

### Notes for expanding this list

- Aim for personalities that **change how you position or spend actions**, not ones that just
  add a number. `cautious`, `dutiful` and `counter` are the good models; `stubborn` is the
  boring one.
- A personality that **reacts to events on the map** is worth two that only work in battle.
  `greedy` and `kind` are the seeds of that — extend in that direction.
- The RimWorld goal (concept §11b) is that a personality eventually grants **one unique battle
  action**. Nothing here does yet; that is the next step for this file.
