5. The money, honestly

First, a correction to v1. Since #55 the post-battle haul auto-draws one random row from that fight's spoils rows, and most of those rows pay in gear or salvage, not coin. Expected crowns per fight, computed from the live LOOT tables:

clash 10   brigand 11   pack 15   mother 30   slingline 20   steading 22   snare 19   armour 0

So the whole act pays about 127 crowns of fight loot on the richest route, not the 300-odd v1's table implied. Fights pay in iron and gear; the purse is fed by the opening advance and by events. That is worth knowing before tuning anything.

What this says. At 90 the muster is a real shop: you hire and buy one cheap piece and walk away with something. At today's 48 you can do one of the two and that is the whole reason the number has to move. The purse still floors twice, at Coldharrow and at the Last Muster, which is correct: the pillar is that the road keeps offering you things you cannot pay for, and it now does that after the shopping rather than instead of it.

Cost of removing the pass: 4 extra days at 10 to 12 a day, about 45 crowns of wages, which is most of what the bigger start purse buys back. The two changes roughly cancel, so the act is no richer than today. If it plays too tight, the single constant to move is the wagon, and it goes in #50's parking lot rather than being retuned mid-session.

The safe route costs 2 more days (~20 crowns) and gives up the Broken Men's 11 crowns and their scars, buying 2 extra slot events instead. The quiet road on fork B gives up the Stone Field's 20 crowns and Bonepicker's, for one slot and no fight. That last one is the sharpest trade on the map: the fight road pays you and then lets you refit, the quiet road does neither and costs you nobody.

6. Rule changes, one at a time

6.1 Road events: half as often, at most one per leg

Today: 1 to 3 stops per leg, 55% chance of something at each. Proposed: 28% per stop, and a latch so the rest of the leg stays quiet once something has fired (a returner counts).

chance of at least one thing on a leg      today    proposed
  1 day                                     0.55      0.28
  2 days                                    0.80      0.48
  3+ days                                   0.91      0.63
expected road events, full risky route      ~9.4      ~4.7

Exactly half, and a longer road is still more likely to have something on it, so 8f.51's "a longer road is more road" survives. The vignette/incident mix (55% and 82% on later stops) is untouched.

6.2 The Thing in Armour re-anchors, and it has to

Today's rule is "the first ordinary arrival after the first muster". With the muster moved before the Fen, that rule fires the ambush on arrival at the Black Fen, on top of the boss. The rule must travel with the muster. Proposed: the anchor is the node where the fork after Coldharrow rejoins, computed at boot from branchOf('vill').join, so no coordinate and no id is hardcoded and a future map rebuild cannot silently move it. On this map that is The Dead Company, reached by both roads. Everything else about it is unchanged: once a run, on arrival, the node's own event queues behind it.

⚠ branchOf reads EDGES, so the anchor must be resolved after the EDGES declaration, never in a boot IIFE above it. That exact temporal-dead-zone crash already cost this project a session.

6.3 The armourer

The rack leaves Coldharrow and stands at the muster as the quartermaster's cart. Same rule, three pieces on the boards, buy one, he will not sell you two. The early rack rolls from the cheap row only: buckler 55 · quiver 50 · shield 60 · shortsw 65 · spear 75 · jack 95. Coldharrow keeps one expensive piece instead of three cheap ones, rolled from mail 210 · bow 110 · maul 150 · focus 80, so the town is still where a big purse buys something a battered company cannot.

6.4 The start purse

Wagon 48 → 90, inherited wagon 24 → 48. The prologue is untouched, so the opening is 120 / 150 / 180 depending on how you answered the man in the coat. The boot line "Forty-eight crowns, and don't" becomes "Ninety crowns, and don't".

6.5 Slots and the pool

Seven slots: b1 f1 f2 f3 q1 e1 p1. A single run walks 2 to 5 of them. The floating pool drops to 16 because Bonepicker's Camp becomes fixed; nothing else leaves and nothing is added. No splitting by road, per 8.6.

6.6 The Roadside Fire

fire, t:'camp', fixed, between the Broken Men and the Ruined Steading. It is not a new event. It opens one card from the existing CAMPS deck, the same deck the road already rolls at random, and then the company walks on.

Why it is worth a node rather than a slot. Three reasons, in order of weight:

  1. It repairs what 8.7 costs. Halving the road-event rate halves how often the fire-side deck comes up, and that deck is where the chains live: debt → debt2/3/4, kin → kin2/3/4, fiddler, found. Those chains are the only content in the game that does all six links (named person, witnessed choice, mechanical act, stored fact, delayed consequence, changed encounter). Thinning them by half and pinning one to the map is a better trade than either alone.
  2. Two fights were one day apart. The Broken Men and the dogs, with nothing between them. That is a check the dramaturge runs and the front half was the only place still failing it.
  3. It rhymes with what comes next, on purpose. You make camp and the company talks. The next night you make camp inside a ruined palisade and are hit through the wall. Two camps in a row, one where nothing happens and one where everything does. The quiet one is what makes the second one land.

Days: paid for, not added. clash → b1 drops from 2 days to 1, so fork A's risky road is still 4 days against the safe road's 6, and the run is still 17 to 21. The risky road now reads fight, slot, fight, fire, fight.

Edge case for implementation: the camp deck is once-per-id per run, so if it were ever empty the node must fall through to a vignette rather than to an empty card. Early in the act it cannot be empty, but the fallback still gets written.

The two I did not pick, and why. A plain slot would have been cheapest, but the risky road already has one and a second says nothing about the road. Pulling the wedding out of the floating pool would have guaranteed the warmest card in the game, and made ratkin legible as people before the Snare, but it makes one road strictly nicer and takes the best card away from every other road in every other run.

8. Flags: things I decided that you may want to overrule

  1. 8.3 read as "both". Your "Да" answered an either/or. I moved the rack to the muster and left Coldharrow one expensive piece. If you meant "move it entirely, Coldharrow gets nothing", delete section 6.3's last sentence and the town drops to three things.
  2. The recovery beat is Bonepicker's Camp, not new prose. It buys food, ogre armour, a gem sale and one restful night for +14 morale, but it does not close wounds. If you want actual healing there before the ambush, that is one new row on an existing event, which is your event session, not mine.
  3. The act is 4 days longer at the short end (17 rather than 13). That is the price of deleting the pass, and it is paid in wages. The cheapest single day to buy back is the e1 slot on fork C; say the word and it goes.
  4. The lore line about the Hunch needs one sentence changed. README says the road "cannot cross except at one pass". With the pass deleted the crossing is the north shoulder and the return is round the southern foot, which is what the map already draws. Small doc edit, not a design question.
