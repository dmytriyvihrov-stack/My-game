THE RUBRIC — what the numbers mean
DRAMA is a signed move, not a level. Positive winds tension up — threat, an irreversible cost, a moral compromise, a name put at risk. Negative discharges it — relief, warmth, competence, comedy. A story that only goes up numbs an audience as fast as one that never moves; the alternation is the point.

+5 the act's climax · +4 a named monster · +3 a real fight, or a crime you cannot undo · +2 teeth shown, a hard ruling · +1 unease, a question, a small cost · 0 texture · −1 a good trade, competence · −2 warmth, comedy · −3 full release: the fire, the wedding

SWING is how far the player's choice can move that number. 0 = the beat is the beat. 3 = the choice changes what kind of story it is (buy from the pedlar, or take his cart).

LORE and CHARACTER are +1 each, and only ever climb.

EROSION (0–3) is how hard the cruel option pays — not whether one exists. 3 is "the profitable answer is the ugly one". It is the pillar made checkable: you cannot afford to be good to everyone.

FOUR CURVES
SURVIVAL PRESSURE is not scored — it is the ledger. Crowns and provisions against wages per day, including the days the road itself cost. A longer road is only dramatic if it takes something, so the tool spends the real numbers out of the game file rather than asking you how it felt.
ATTACHMENT counts your character flag plus what the data already knows is about a person: a choice that scars somebody, a card gated on a bond.
LORE and EROSION as above.

Switch played between kind and cruel and watch the same road stop hurting. That gap is the game.

WHAT KIND OF CONTENT — derived, not typed
MAIN carries the main thread · SIDE carries any other thread, i.e. it continues something · DYN carries none, so it is about nothing yet and is poolable. Give a beat a thread and it stops being dynamic — that is the same gesture as writing it into a chain.

◇ ANY — the slot cards at the top of the tray are not events. Drop one on the line and it stands for "the game draws a merchant thing here", scored as the mean of that pool. Plan the shape in slots, fill it with named events after.

⚔ tier on a card is its battle tier; ⚔~ means dynamic — generated rather than authored. A dynamic fight is balanced as a band, so the teaching fight and the climax must never be one.

WHAT IT IS FOR

The dramaturge board is what you intended. This tab is what people actually did — which roads they took, which choices, and where they pressed ★ ▲ ▼.

THE INPUT ALREADY EXISTS

Backlog #53 shipped the reaction bar, the decision log, the per-run report and a copy-paste blob so a friend's journal travels home. That blob is this tab's input format. Nothing new has to be built in the game — a playtester finishes a run, copies their journal, and pastes it here.

⚠ #53's invariant #10: the journal observes and never acts. This tab inherits that — it reads blobs and never writes to a save.

WHAT IT SHOULD DRAW

  • Path heat — the map with every road weighted by how many runs took it. The roads nobody takes are the real finding.
  • Choice split — per event, the share each option took. An option under ~5% is either badly written or badly priced.
  • Reaction against intent — ★▲▼ per beat plotted against the drama score you gave it here. Beats you scored high that nobody reacted to are the list to rewrite.
  • Where runs end — day and place of every death and every quit.
  • Felt curve vs designed curve — the ▲▼ stream as its own tension line, laid over the one on the DRAMATURGE tab. That overlay is the whole reason both tabs live in one file.
