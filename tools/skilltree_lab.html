BATTLE BROTHERS · the full perk tree

How it works: one perk point per level, 11 levels, 44 perks. A row does not unlock by level: row N+1 opens after N points are spent. No prerequisites inside the tree. You finish with about 10 of 44, so every pick hurts. (Source: the Perks page on the BB wiki, checked 2026-08-12.)

THE LAST SPELL · summary

Shape: the attacks live on the WEAPON. Each weapon carries its own 2 to 4 actives; swap the weapon and the hero's whole move set changes. The hero levels into PASSIVES only: every level deals a small random hand of perks, you keep one, rerolls are a scarce resource. Rarer perks start appearing at higher levels.
What to steal: weapon-owned attacks (already our rule), and the dealt hand: two heroes of the same build still end up different. (Written from memory, not fetched. Correct me where it is off.)

WILDERMYTH · the full ability list

How it works: three callings. Each starts with one signature move (warrior GUARDIAN overwatch, hunter SILKSTEP stealth, mystic INTERFUSE object-bonding). On every promotion the game deals about four abilities: one class non-upgrade and one upgrade guaranteed. You keep ONE. Rerolls cost Legacy points and each reroll costs more. Every ability has a "+" version that is only offered if you own the base. The wild things (wolf limbs, crow wings, fire arms) never sit in the tree: EVENTS graft them on, and each theme brings its own upgrades (Fly, Howl, Meteor Strike ...).
What to steal: the dealt hand with one guaranteed class pick, and event-gated transformations: that is exactly our BATTLE-MAGE door. (Source: wildermyth.com/wiki/Abilities, checked 2026-08-12.)

WARTALES · classes, one skeleton

How it works: the attacks come from the weapon and class; every companion climbs the SAME level skeleton. Level 2: pick a valor generator or your first strike. Level 3: pick a SPECIALIZATION: it renames the job and grants its signature skill. Levels 5 and 8: one passive of four. Level 10: one utility of three (SECOND WEAPON is offered to everyone). Level 12: a "bravery" skill plus CLASS SPECIALISATION (upgrade one earlier skill). Level 15: MASTER CLASS (a new specialization opens). Valor is a shared party pool that these passives feed.
What to steal: the level 3 specialization asks the class question once, out loud, and never again; fixed pick-levels keep choices rare and heavy. (Source: the Wartales fandom wiki, checked 2026-08-12. Two classes are still "tba" there.)

BANNER SAGA · one passive, one active

How it works: there is no tree of picks. A hero is a BASE CLASS (one always-on passive) promoted into a SUB-CLASS (one active ability). Promotions raise the active's rank 1 to 3: same trick, bigger. At rank 6 the hero gains a SECOND active from a sibling sub-class, and that is the only branching choice in the game. Stats are five numbers (Armor, Strength, Willpower, Exertion, Break) with per-class caps, and Strength is both your health and your damage.
What to steal: one passive plus one active keeps a twelve-body roster readable at a glance, and the borrowed sibling trick at rank 6 is a late reward that adds no new system. Stat caps are the real class identity. (Source: the Banner Saga wiki, checked 2026-08-12.)
Rules of the draft:
1. One skill point per level, starting at level 2. Level 1 gives the race's faction skill free.
2. Five lanes: 1 class and armor · 2 class skills · 3 race · 4 and 5 general. Levels run DOWNWARD. Level 9 tops out ordinary growth and LEVEL 10 is the capstone: one choice, multiclass or mastery. Some levels are empty on purpose.
3. The weapon owns the attacks (spear: THRUST and SPEAR WALL). The tree buys passives, stances and unlocks only.
4. The CLASS is the level 2 door, and the door IS the weapon mastery: the brute swings club and axe alike. The door then GRANTS its two class skills free at levels 3 and 6; only the class's level 9 skill is bought, and it carries a real downside. One door per life (until the level 10 capstone).
5. Level 5 offers ARMOR MASTERY: one of three, once.
6. BATTLE-MAGE and BARD crown the tree at level 9. Battle-mage is never a pick (the road gives it, events only); bard is parked for later.
Migration note: in the live game SPEAR WALL belongs to the spearwoman and AIMED SHOT to the archer (their sig). Under rule 3 the stance moves to the weapon itself, every spear-holder gets it, and the class door keeps the competence (+5 with the family weapon and one sweetener). Same migration for BACKSTAB (knife) and SWEEP (club).
