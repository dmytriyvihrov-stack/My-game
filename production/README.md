# Production

This folder contains the business and delivery side of Grimtoll rather than files loaded by the game.

Keep here:

- budgets and cost models;
- schedules and production plans;
- Steam and platform compliance notes;
- outsourcing briefs, quotes and contracts;
- pitch, funding and release-planning material;
- production graphs and decision reports.

Keep runtime art in `art/`, authored game content in `content/`, design specifications in `docs/`, and the playable build in `prototype/`.

This separation makes production decisions easy to find and share without mixing reports or commercial documents into the game asset pipeline.
