Five calls the board is waiting on, and all five are yours

Five others were made on 2 Aug and are already built or banked: the under-hill tunnel stays a secret, the dog pack stays the keep-your-ground skill check, provisions stay heal-only, the noble pays a third more, and both reported text bugs are fixed.

Does the polish gate outrank the deep work? The buckets above assume yes: five entries before the friends play, everything structural after. If you would rather interleave, the honest cost is that a friend meets a build in the middle of a rule change.

Is #50 pulled forward at playtest grade? It is marked deliberately last and bucket 1 puts it second. The recommendation keeps the number and keeps the terminal pass: classes, races and fights get tuned to survivable and fair, and the deferred pieces it holds stay behind for the real one.

Is the spearwoman sayable in one sentence without #47? That is the one row I moved out of bucket 1 on a judgement. It is a rework, not an addition, but it touches enemy AI in both brains, which is not what you want destabilised the week before you hand the build out. If the class pass finds her unsayable, #47 comes forward.

Does fleeing the Thing in Armour keep deleting the Dead Company? Deliberate in code, but THE LONG FIRE one node later still mentions the nine men in the ditch to a player who never met them. Three one-line answers in the QA report, and one of them is just a text edit you could make during the event-text pass.

Do the bells ring over every dead company? The defeat card opens with "The bells are still ringing" wherever the run ends, including half a map from Grausen. Keep it placeless, or key one clause on where they fell.
