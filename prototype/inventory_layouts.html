THE COMPANY SCREEN · four layouts

All four are drawn at the game's real 1280×720, from the build's own tokens and #187's aftermath furniture (the medallion, the chip, the tray), so the only thing that changes between them is where the room goes. Today's split is roster 224 · character 606 · stash 450, and the character column is the one carrying the doll. Nothing here is wired; the data is real so the boxes are the right size.

A · THE RAIL STASH 450 → 726 · PERKS 0 → A REAL PANEL HIDDEN: the doll · the XP ring · wages · gear stat deltas

The smallest move from today, and the one with the least risk. The roster keeps its column but loses everything except a face and a name; the character column loses the doll entirely and states what is worn as a six-medallion strip, which is the same information in one row instead of a picture. Everything that row frees goes to the stash and to a perk panel that finally has somewhere to live.

B · TWO TRAYS GEAR 596 · PERKS 596, SIDE BY SIDE HIDDEN: the doll · the roster column · the trait paragraph

The biggest departure and the one that answers the ask most directly. The person becomes a thin band across the top - face, name, four stat chips, one trait line - and the whole body of the screen is two equal trays: everything you can wear on the left, everything they can learn on the right. The roster moves into that band as a row of faces. Equipped and stash are in the SAME tray, so moving a thing is one space rather than a journey across the screen.

C · STASH IS THE SCREEN STASH 850 AS A GRID · PERKS SHARE THE SAME AREA HIDDEN: the doll · stats behind a hover · the roster column

For the way the screen is actually used between fights, which is sorting loot. The stash stops being a list and becomes a grid of cards filling most of the screen; PERKS is a tab on that same big area, so both of the things you said need room get all of it, one at a time. The wearer is a narrow left column of slots, and the roster is a rail of faces along the bottom.

D · THE FOLIO GEAR + PERKS INSIDE ONE CARD, 968 WIDE HIDDEN: the doll · everything not on the open person

The one that looks most like the after-battle screen, because it is built the same way: one card per person with the emblem and the rays behind it, and gear and perks sitting side by side inside that card rather than in separate furniture. The roster is a left rail of faces. It is the prettiest of the four and the least flexible: a card has a fixed shape, so a person with nine perks and a person with two look more alike than they should.
