# Sanya - playtest report

## Verdict
A hex-grid tactics game about a broke posh guy who gets a pint poured on his head and has to fight his way out of a tavern, and apparently ends up leading whoever joins him. It looks like Battle Brothers from the streams I have seen (little pixel men, armour and hitpoints bars, faces for morale) but it holds your hand a lot more in the first fight, in a good way. Fourteen actions in I have only seen one fight and its tutorial tips, no map, no money, no road. Would I play again: yes. The one reason: killing Harl in three swings and then having his three mates walk in felt like a proper story beat, not a menu.

## Where I stopped and why
Stop rule 3: the tester brief called time on the dry run. I did not choose to stop. The concrete moment: round III of the tavern brawl, me with my back in the hearth corner, one of the three left standing next to me at 56%, the other two queued behind him, and I had two actions still to spend. I would have kept going.

## Interest over time
| checkpoint | interest 1-5 | what I was doing | why that score |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~13 actions | 4 | in the fire corner after DISENGAGE + MOVE, "THE ROOM IS WATCHING" tip up | the kill felt great and every tip answered a question I actually had, but I had not made one decision that was mine yet |
| final (~14) | 4 | enemy turn passed, one man on me, two stuck behind him | the corner trick worked exactly as promised, which is satisfying; still no road, no money, nothing but the fight |

High point: "KILLED" floating over Harl with a -25, and the top counter flipping from skull 1 to skull 3 as his mates walked in. Low point: the moment the "put your back in a corner" tip appeared AFTER my turn had auto-ended, so I could not act on it and got hit first. Not a big low, but it was the one time I felt a beat behind the game.

## Where I got stuck or confused
1. **Turn ending on its own.** After my move + swing on Harl I did not see the turn end; the round counter just said II when I next looked and I was still at full health. I was trying to work out what Harl did on his turn. I expected to see him swing (miss) or at least a line saying so. What happened: nothing I noticed. It took me until the "ARMOUR" tip a turn later to be sure enemies do act between my turns. There is a BATTLELOG button top left that probably has it, but I did not want to spend a click on it. What would have saved me: a one-line "Harl swings and misses" near his head, or the log auto-opening on the first enemy action.
2. **The tip that came too late.** "THREE ON ONE ... Put your back in one" showed up on the kill, which was my second action, so my turn was over. They closed on me and hit me before I could do what it said. I got past it next turn by spending my whole turn on DISENGAGE + MOVE, no attack, which felt bad. What would have saved me: show that tip at the START of my next turn, or give me the corner move as part of the tutorial before they act.
3. **The camera moving between turns.** Twice the board zoomed/panned by itself (once after the three came in, once when I reached the corner). Each time I lost my guy for a second and had to re-find the star. Never a real block, just a "wait, where am I" every time. A slower pan or keeping my man in the same screen spot would fix it.
4. **What "68%" and "13%" on my own panel are.** Two little icons with percentages under my portrait, and a "?" button next to them. I never clicked the "?" (saving actions). I guessed 68% is my hit chance (it matched the number over Harl) and 13% is... dodge? Never found out. Not stuck, just an unread number.

## What I understood, and what I did not
- **Movement** - UNDERSTOOD. "Click a green hex to walk", MOVE card says 4 HEXES, count went to 3 after a step, UNDO MOVE appeared. Clear.
- **Attacking** - UNDERSTOOD. Click a man, a % and a damage range float over him first, then "-6 arm" then "-11" then "KILLED". Armour first, then hitpoints; the "Iron soaks a blow before flesh does" tip confirmed it.
- **Actions per turn** - UNDERSTOOD. "Two actions a turn", two diamonds under ACTIONS, each card shows its diamond cost (HOLD THE LINE costs two). The turn ended by itself when both were spent, which I only realised after the fact (see stuck 1).
- **Enemy behaviour / what they will do** - PARTLY. When I clicked Harl I got "WHAT IT CAN DO TO YOU: CUDGEL 7-13 rch 1, KICK 2-4 rch 1", and the red-edged hexes around him I read as his reach. That is more than I expected. But I never saw WHICH of those he was going to do or at whom, and I never actually saw him do anything. So I know his menu, not his intent.
- **Hit chance breakdown** - PARTLY. Hovering showed "68% CHANCE TO HIT, your skill +68, their dodge: footwork +10, nerve wavering -2, drunk -8, attack arc FRONT". I got the shape (drunk = easier to hit, front vs side matters). The small lines are small.
- **Nerve / morale** - PARTLY. Faces over heads and words on my panel: HAPPY, IT ROCKS after the kill, back to HAPPY after I got hit; Harl went from a neutral face and OK to a worried face after a -21. I get that it goes up and down with the fight. I do not know what happens when it bottoms out (do they run?). Guess: they run.
- **Disengage / free hits** - UNDERSTOOD, and I guessed it before the tip. "Moving while they touch you pays a free swing to each of them." DISENGAGE gave "CLEAN +1 HEX" and has a "READY IN 2 TURNS" cooldown.
- **Corners / positioning** - UNDERSTOOD, and proven: in the hearth corner only one of three could reach me.
- **Drunk status** - UNDERSTOOD, from the mug badge and its tip.
- **Money, wages, food, gear, days, resting, road events, the map** - NO. Never reached any of it. Nothing on the fight screen mentions any of them.
- **KICK / COMMAND / HOLD THE LINE cards** - NO. Saw the names and costs (KICK 2-4 PUSH, COMMAND RALLY, HOLD THE LINE GUARDS ALLIES), did not use them; HOLD THE LINE went grey once I had moved, I guess because it needs a full turn.

## The fights
One fight, the tavern. It felt good: the swings land with a number and a face change, three hits and Harl was on the floor. Did I know what enemies could do before they did it: half. The game showed me Harl's two moves and their damage when I clicked him, and red hexes around him, so I knew his reach; that is better than most, but not the Into the Breach thing where I would see "he is hitting THIS hex for THIS much". Did I have a plan or was I clicking: the first turn was a plan (step in, swing). Turns two and three the game gave me the plan through tips and I followed it. The one time it felt slightly unfair was the tip about corners arriving after my turn was spent, so I ate a hit; once I understood the turn had auto-ended it was fair, just badly timed. Three on one felt right, not hopeless, because the corner made it one on one.

## Reading and looking
The intro card was five short paragraphs; I read three properly, that is about the ceiling. The tip boxes were the right length, two or three lines each, but there were six of them in the first fight and by the fifth I was reading them like a manual. The hover breakdown over an enemy is a lot of small print (11px lines like "their footwork +10, nerve wavering -2, drunk -8"): useful, but I only skimmed it. Numbers over the enemies' heads (56%, 18-26) are 8px and I had to lean in on the screenshot to be sure. Never had trouble finding a button. The floating hitpoint/armour bars over heads are thin but readable.

## Three things I would fix first
1. Show me what the enemy did on his turn: a line by his head or the log opening itself the first time. I went a whole round not knowing whether Harl had swung.
2. Time the "put your back in a corner" tip so I can actually do it, at the start of my turn, not the moment my last action lands.
3. Calm the camera between turns; move it slower or keep my man where he was.

## Something I liked
Killing Harl and having the top counter go from skull 1 to skull 3 as his friends walked in from the yard. And "On this island, most would rather watch an unfairness than join a fight that already looks lost" is a good line and I want to see who stands up.
