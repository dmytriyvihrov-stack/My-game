The Four Ladders

Every stat is nine signed rungs, −4 to +4, where 0 is the ordinary human and each rung says what it gives or costs. Every number on this page below the givens is computed by the page itself out of the givens, the race table and the ladders. The only thing authored on a body is its four lines – born, race, class, trait – and the weapon in its hands. Change a rung, a race row or a born value and the company recomputes. None of this is in the game yet.

The shape one multiplication; everything else is one bracket that adds

The givens the ordinary human, before any rung

These are the roster’s and the enemy does not move with them. Every foe takes its hitpoints, skill and dodge from its own template, so a raised roster baseline is structurally roster-only: it is a change to the people you own and to nobody else.

The race the body, one table

Class leans, one rung each: spear STR · brute STR · archer AGI · cutter AGI · mage INT and MOR · captain MOR. A body’s rung on any stat is born + race + class + trait, four named lines, and that is the whole answer to “why is this one stronger”. A bow class and a casting class carry of the race’s hitpoints.

The ladders

THE TOP RUNG COSTS TWO POINTS. A capstone is not a bigger number, it is a rule of its own: one point buys the rung’s numbers and the second buys the rule. It also protects the pace – at one to two stat points an act, +4 arrives late in act 3 and only for a body that spent everything on one stat. The two small empty squares under the +4 digit are the price tag, in the rung’s own number column, so it reads before the rung does. ⚠ No other rung shows squares: one point is the default and only the exception needs a receipt.
WHERE A RUNG IS READ, AND NO DIGIT IS ADDED. The company sheet already prints one word per stat and opens the whole ladder on the stat’s hover in the inventory. That hover is where a rung’s word, its picture and what it gives belong, and it costs the sheet nothing because it is already built. No number goes beside the stat. The digit on this page is the designer’s view, not the player’s.
AN INJURY AND A SCAR ARE TWO DIFFERENT THINGS, AND THE RUNG ONLY TOUCHES ONE. An injury is damage the body is carrying (hpLoss). It closes on the road at a day – the same number DOUBLE RATIONS doubles – so MORALE +3 turns a dial that already exists and needs nothing new built. A scar is the permanent mark (p.injuries: Rat-bite fever, −1 STR), and the sheet labels each one with that exact word. The rung does not touch scars. They stay permanent and the medicine chest, once a run, stays the only thing that takes one off.

Where each rung comes from four named lines · born is the only authored number, and it is editable

Born is 0 unless a backstory has paid for it, and today only the Captain’s has. Everything else on a body is the race row, the class lean and the trait, which is why nothing in the table below is typed. Use the arrows to move a born value and read the company change under it.

The company on it computed, live, from the two tables above

The race is in four of these columns and it is only visible in three. Hitpoints carry it and so does the swing. Dodge does not: the race’s body is a modifier on the ATTACKER’s roll, which is the last column, not on the defender’s dodge. So a ratkin dodges at 18 and everybody rolling at him takes −5, instead of folding that −5 into his dodge and printing 23. Same arithmetic, said in the honest place: a small body is hard to hit because it is small, not because it is nimble.

Swing is the weapon’s dice through the bracket, before nerve. To land is the spell number and only the caster spends it. Nerve is the pool, not what is left of it.

What changes in the engine per stat, in the file’s own terms

  • A roster body stops carrying its own numbers. makeParty authors four rung lines and a weapon; finishRecruit derives hitpoints, to hit, dodge, nerve and the swing from them, the way this page does. There is no per-body hitpoint literal left to drift.
  • STRENGTH and AGILITY. D.hp becomes RACE[r].hp × class × (1 + held(STR,'hp')); the flat round(0.3×STR) is deleted and baked into every weapon and enemy act row (+2 both ends); D.hit becomes 70 + held(AGI,'hit') and D.dodge 14 + held(AGI,'dodge'); RACEDODGE and SIZEHIT collapse into the race table’s to be hit column.
  • INTELLECT. arcSkill becomes 69 + held(INT,'land'); the dodge against a spell goes to zero at both sites that carry it today (dodgeOf(d)×0.35 in the breakdown and in the nerve row) – a spell lands or it does not; D.spell becomes 1 + held(INT,'magic') with the spell dice re-based once so rung 0 casts what INT 9 casts today; D.learn becomes 1 + held(INT,'xp'); D.flank becomes a flat 15 and stops reading INT; reach is spellRange and act.range +1.
  • MORALE. D.moral becomes 90 + held(MOR,'nerve'); MORALE.recover becomes 8 + held(MOR,'climb'); every negative mor(u,amt) multiplies by 1 + held(MOR,'loss') at the one site that pays them; an injury heals at MEND_BASE × (1 + held(MOR,'heal')), the same dial double rations already turns; scars are not touched; D.bonus is deleted and the nerve rung carries the damage instead (HAPPY +5%, IT ROCKS +10%).
  • The four capstones are rules, not numbers, and each is read somewhere else. STANDS is a once-a-fight floor at the site that applies damage; FLEET is +1 on step; UNREPEATABLE is a per-turn flag on the defender, cleared at the top of each turn and read where a target is chosen, so both AI brains obey it without being taught twice; MORALE’s is a promotion rule and lands in the level-up screen.

Still open ideas with a place to go, not decisions

  • The heavy blow. STR +3: once a fight, one blow at ×1.5, chosen. A button, not a roll.
  • MORALE’s capstone is the one that is a promotion and not a battlefield rule, so it is the odd one of the four. Two perk points is worth the same as the other three; it just never shows on the board.
  • The map’s mood, printed and symmetric. Today only a bad mood reaches a fight, silently. Proposal: 😄 +8 nerve at the start of every fight · 🙂 +4 · 😐 0 · 😟 −4 · 💀 −8, said on the chip’s hover.
  • Jack of all trades. The stranger who never picks a trade learns from whoever he stands beside; a perk from any tier-1 pool at each level, and the adjacency the board already measures decides which stat he gains.
  • The backstory, unfolding. Three chapters a body, opened by a level, a named fight or a road card; the third pays a rung of born. The Captain’s is already spent: born +1 STR and +1 AGI.
  • The word. The game calls a spell a working in 22 player-facing places. Renaming it to magic is a read-every-hit job, not a replace: nine more uses of the same word are ordinary English and must survive.

Build order

  • Pass one: STR and AGI rungs, the race table, the class leans, the raised 60 and 70, the founders’ four lines, the flat baked into the weapon rows, the two-point capstone and the promotion that refuses it. The sheet keeps the word it prints and the hover it already opens; no digit is added. INT and MOR keep today’s wiring.
  • Pass two: INT and MOR onto the same scaffolding, the spell dodge to zero, the mend dial, UNREPEATABLE.
  • The gate, both passes: ARENA.match() over the eight authored fights, n=50 a side, before and after, read as win rate and as the fraction of runs under a threshold – the raised baseline is a real buff on every body you own and has to be priced; the sheet’s overflow probe against HEAD; LINT() at 0.
