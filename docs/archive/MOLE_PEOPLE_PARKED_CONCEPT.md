# Mole people — parked concept

**Status:** parked on 2026-08-18. Not canon, not in the current backlog, and not approved for implementation.

## Core fiction

Humanoid subterranean creatures provisionally called **mole people / burrowers**. Some are fully
blind and navigate only by vibration; others see poorly at short range. They should remain visually
distinct from ratkin: broad low bodies, no tail, sealed or vestigial eyes, long digging hands, dark
velvet fur and heavy shoulder/back silhouettes.

## Parked encounter shape

- **Large male / Deepback:** much larger and stronger, slow, normally protected by extremely heavy
  armour or overlapping body plates. After two ordinary turns he enters a clearly telegraphed
  **LISTEN** pose. The pose changes his silhouette and partially animates the body; while listening,
  the armour is temporarily inactive and the exposed body is vulnerable. Listening would also let
  him identify and pursue the loudest recent position, so it is both a weakness and a useful act.
- **Smaller female / Dim-eye:** faster, lightly armoured and partially sighted. She can correct what
  she hears at short range, while distant movement is read only through sound. A future version may
  use short underground repositioning rather than another ordinary melee skill.
- **Three blind young / Blindlings:** exact combat role unresolved. The leading idea is support
  rather than damage: follow loud movement, attach to or mark noisy bodies, and flee through forced
  movement or after the adults fall. Killing them should not be required to win.

## Mechanic deliberately deferred

A readable sound layer would be required: movement and attacks leave visible vibration markers;
fully blind creatures navigate to markers, while dim-sighted creatures see actual bodies only at
short range. This touches battlefield UI, AI, action previews, animation, terrain and encounter
balance, so it is too complex for the current build and must not be implemented piecemeal.

## Revisit test

Return to this only if playtesting still shows that the existing human, ratkin and ogre formations
do not create enough different tactical answers after their art, equipment and encounter
compositions are properly expressed.
