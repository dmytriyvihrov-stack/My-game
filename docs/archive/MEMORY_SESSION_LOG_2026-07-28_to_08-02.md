## 2026-08-01 · **#60 SHIPPED — the first battle teaches itself** (build log 8f.62, re-cut in 8f.63)

Ten lessons in the Captain's mouth, fired the first time each rule bites, once ever on
`LEGACY.seen`: sides · walk at them · the second action · the archer · the caster · go round the
back · they broke · **the zone of control** · **DISENGAGE** · **your own nerve**. Everything about
it is in `CAPLINES` and `docs/CHANGELOG.md`; what is worth carrying to another feature:

- ⚑ **A LESSON QUEUES WHERE A COMMENT DROPS, and that is the whole design.** #51's drop rule was
  written because *"a comment about a moment that has passed is worse than silence"* — **that
  reasoning does not cover a rule**, which is still true next round. Same logic made lessons the only
  thing that **survives the Captain going down** (a rule is not his opinion) and the only tier
  outside the five-a-battle budget. **Before adding a rule to a register, ask what its tier is FOR.**
- ⚑ **THE REGISTER HAS TWO VOICES AND THE TIER IS WHICH ONE YOU ARE IN**, set by the user across
  three messages: *"a bit less epic, more clear he says"* → *"For me important - that people
  understand. And then the regular capitan comands more in a vibe of game."* A **lesson** says the
  rule, says what to do, stops, **and may name a mechanic out loud** (a player who does not know the
  word cannot look it up). A **comment** stays atmospheric. Two tests, both in the code: a lesson
  must still teach with the balloon deleted; a comment must still work as film dialogue.
- ⚑ **`CAPTIER[E.tier]||1` PROMOTED EVERY LESSON TO TEACH**, because the new tier's value is `0` and
  `0||1` is 1. **A new tier whose value is falsy is a trap any `||` default will spring**, and this
  file leans on that idiom everywhere.
- ⚑ **WHEN YOU SHARPEN ONE LINE IN A REGISTER, CHECK THE LINE THAT FIRES NEXT TO IT.** Re-cutting the
  zone line to *"you get a free hit"* made the DISENGAGE line the same rule twice, a tick apart.
  Fixed as **9 is the problem, 8 is the answer**.
- ⚑ **A RECEIPT IS NOT AN OFFER.** `w_back` was deleted because it rode `whisper('back')`, which
  fires *after* a blow lands in a back — congratulating the player on something they had worked out.
  The user wanted it one turn earlier, while the movement is unspent.
- ⚑ **TEACH THE GAME THAT EXISTS.** There is **no hard Zone of Control rule** (#29 parked, cut for
  making movement unaffordable). The line describes a **price on leaving**, never a prohibition;
  *"you are pinned"* would cause the exact failure that got the rule cut. #29 must rewrite it.
- **Deleted with it:** `weBend` (*"it fired on two or more of ours off steady, which is most of the
  middle of most fights"* — **a trigger that can fire in a typical round is not a moment**) and the
  **`coach('fight')` card**, user: *"I prefer tutorial as capitan says, rather than in unit card"*.
  Register entry removed too, because **a dead entry is a second source of truth**.

**What a later session needs to know about the code:**
- `TERRAIN{}` (8 places) + `FIELDS{}` (which fight is fought where) + `fieldFor()` merges them.
  `SIM.ground` overrides the kind, behind `SIM.on` only.
- **`BLOCKED` gained `tree` · `huge`/`hugeA` · `wall` · `flower`, and `tree`/`wall` are
  deliberately NOT `rock`** — #46's `loneRock` tests `==='rock'` exactly, so keeping the kinds
  apart IS the mechanism stopping an ogre uprooting a forest or dismantling a fort. **Any new
  impassable kind must decide on purpose whether an ogre may lift it.**
- **`nerveFrac()` is a new single site** every ladder read goes through (`restate`, `ladderAt`,
  `rocking`). **#32's rally rule wants that function, not `u.morale`.**
- `moveBudget` now carries three ground rules; `terrainCheck()` validates a board (spawn only).
- `LAYOUT_KEEP` — a hand-built layout reserves its openings and every generator asks.

**The three lessons worth carrying out of it:**
- ⚑ **TUNE BY MEASUREMENT, AND BE WILLING TO FAIL YOUR OWN RULE.** The swamp's first density left a
  company at **51% of its plains reach** against a 60% floor the spec had set for itself, and it
  was caught by running `reachMap` over every hex rather than by looking at it. Shipped at 61-64%.
  ⚠ That meant **a measured refusal of the literal request**: "a lot of water ewherywhere" is
  carried by the palette, not by coverage, because at the coverage the words describe the fight
  stops being playable. Say so out loud when you do this.
- ⚑ **THE VALIDATOR EARNED ITSELF ON ITS FIRST RUN.** `terrainCheck()` found that 1 outpost seed in
  24 had a tree planted in the gate, dropping the board to choke 1 — the exact failure the outpost
  was designed to avoid, reintroduced by a generator that had never heard of the outpost. It would
  have shipped, because it is rare and reads as a hard fight rather than a broken one.
- ⚑ **TWO SPEC PREDICTIONS WERE WRONG IN THE CODEBASE'S FAVOUR, and both were found by reading
  before writing.** The bloom's damage penalty was specced as "TWO sites, `strike()` and
  `dmgPreview()`, and this project has already paid for forgetting one" — but `dmgMul` is already
  the single function both call, so it was one site. And the nerve lift wanted a new `nerveFrac()`
  rather than the modifier plumbing the spec imagined. **Read the site before promising the shape
  of the fix.**
