# Grimtoll — Ukrainian Market Research

Independent marketing workspace for the Ukrainian gaming audience.

Research snapshot: **1 August 2026**  
Working title: **Grimtoll**  
Current product language: **English**

## Decision in one sentence

Prepare the game technically for Ukrainian now, begin private relationship-building with exact-fit communities once the demo is testable, and delay the broad Ukrainian-language campaign until there is a stable public demo and a small native-written creator kit.

## Files

- [`UKRAINIAN_MARKET_AND_COMMUNITY_RESEARCH.md`](UKRAINIAN_MARKET_AND_COMMUNITY_RESEARCH.md) — market evidence, community and creator analysis, expected influence, priorities, and risks.
- [`UKRAINE_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md`](UKRAINE_OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md) — milestone-based execution plan, message templates, playtest questions, and measurement.
- [`CREATORS_AND_COMMUNITIES.csv`](CREATORS_AND_COMMUNITIES.csv) — sortable working list with priority, fit, access, role, timing, links, and caveats.

## How to use this folder

1. Read the research report before contacting anyone.
2. Filter the CSV to `A1` for the first closed-test wave.
3. Use the playbook only after its relevant readiness gate is satisfied.
4. Recheck audience numbers and contact rules 7–14 days before outreach. They are snapshots, not forecasts.

## Important boundaries

- “Ukrainian audience” does not mean “Ukrainian developer.” Do not describe Grimtoll as a Ukrainian game unless the team genuinely qualifies.
- Do not use Russian as a substitute for Ukrainian. Use English until Ukrainian copy or localization is ready.
- Do not mass-post Discord invitations or keys. Ask administrators and creators first.
- Do not recruit unpaid fan translators as the default localization plan.
- Never use the war or charity as a marketing angle. Any charitable action must be real, transparent, and independent of coverage.
